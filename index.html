About AMEN Kenya

About us

The first seed was set on 20th July 2019 at a convocation of medics from different institutions, themed From Medical Practice to Medical Evangelism.

Our mission

To train, equip and motivate Adventist medics to share Christ in their marketplace.

Our vision

The equipping of Seventh-day Adventists to be effective medical evangelists. Christ's methods of loving service through the ministry of healing will naturally open hearts to the gospel and the truths of the Bible.

Achievements

  1. Over 20 medical camps across the country, offering free medical services to over 8,106 people and leading to 1,123 baptisms.
  2. Registration with the government.
  3. Monthly training programmes and online health evangelism training by LIGHT.
  4. Four annual conferences, this being the 5th.
  5. Partnership with churches and other ministries.
  6. Prison ministry at Thika Women's and Nairobi Remand Prison.
